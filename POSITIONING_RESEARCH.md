# Gainport positioning research

Reviewed 15 November 2026. This is a positioning brief for the landing page, not a forecast of contract revenue.

## Recommended lead offer

**Senior cloud architecture and modernization delivery for Canadian federal programs, sold to prime contractors and their delivery teams.** Lead with hosting and migration decisions, target architecture, platform foundations, and implementation support. Keep delivery pipelines, reliability, and cost optimization as adjacent workstreams. Describe AI as a potential downstream use case that needs sound cloud and data foundations, not as Gainport's principal specialty.

Why this focus:

- The [2024 Government of Canada Application Hosting Strategy](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/2024-application-hosting-strategy.html) emphasizes choosing hosting models by cost effectiveness and business value, modernizing applications, and controlling costs. It explicitly cautions that cloud is one option among data centre and hybrid approaches. This supports a practical architecture and modernization offer.
- A [2025 Elections Canada cloud platform contract](https://search.open.canada.ca/contracts/record/elections,C-2025-2026-Q2-00018) described planning, architecture, design, implementation, and support for a cloud-based case management platform. Its published value was $1,724,625, but the record says task authorizations can make actual spending lower. This is evidence of substantial multi-role demand, not a likely contract size for Gainport.
- An [ESDC cloud enterprise professional services contract](https://search.open.canada.ca/contracts/record/esdc-edsc,C-2025-2026-Q3-00064) described helping a department implement a cloud adoption model. Its amended value was $432,225.02. This is closely aligned with senior architecture and adoption support.
- A [2025 ESDC AI assistant contract](https://search.open.canada.ca/contracts/record/esdc-edsc,C-2025-2026-Q1-00146) was valued at $407,830.70. AI work is present, but one award does not establish it as the best entry point for this team. The [federal AI strategy](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/responsible-use-ai/gc-ai-strategy-priority-areas.html) identifies secure, scalable cloud and common data infrastructure as foundations for AI adoption.
- Public Services and Procurement Canada's [procurement guidance](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/pspc-spac/documents/pac/pac-seminar-notes-doing-business-gc.pdf) explicitly points suppliers toward joining prime contractors' supply chains.

The federal contracts search is keyword based and descriptions vary. A few visible awards cannot establish which cloud category has the most wins or highest margins. Published contract values are often ceilings, include amendments, and can cover multiple resources. The recommendation is an inference from **market direction plus Arshad's documented experience**, not a claim of a statistically ranked market opportunity.

## Page decision

The copy is written for a prime contractor with a real requirement: what Gainport can own, how Arshad fits into a delivery team, and what happens in the first call. It avoids generic AI transformation claims, unverified success metrics, and promises of procurement outcomes.
