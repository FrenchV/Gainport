GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of cloud projects does Gainport take on?+

We support on-premises to cloud migrations, cloud architecture, CI/CD pipelines, platform engineering, operations, and technical advisory work. We can help shape a plan or work alongside your team to deliver it.

Can you work with our existing team and technology?+

Yes. We start with your current environment, constraints, and team capabilities. Our goal is to strengthen the systems and people you already have while creating a clear path forward.

How do you approach security and reliability?+

We consider security, resilience, maintainability, and operational ownership from the earliest design conversations. The specific controls and practices depend on your requirements and environment.

Do you only work on large transformations?+

No. Some engagements begin with a focused architecture review or a single delivery bottleneck. We right-size the work to the outcome you need.

What happens in the first conversation?+

We’ll learn about your goals, current challenges, timeline, and stakeholders. Then we can suggest a sensible next step and what an engagement might look like.
