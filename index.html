PRACTICAL DETAILS

Questions before we talk?

Can Gainport support a prime contractor’s existing delivery team?+

Yes. We can join a defined workstream, align to your governance and delivery practices, and work alongside the people already accountable for the program.

What is Gainport’s main focus?+

Cloud platform architecture and modernization: deciding what should move, designing how it should run, and helping deliver the foundation. We also support CI/CD, reliability, and cost reviews around that core work.

Can you start with an assessment rather than a full implementation?+

Yes. A focused review can establish the current state, the key risks, and a practical next step before the scope of implementation is set.

Does Gainport provide AI services?+

We can advise on the cloud architecture, data, security, and delivery foundations an AI initiative may require. We would scope any AI-specific work against the team and expertise needed for that project.

What happens in the first call?+

We discuss your organization, the requirement or roadblock, the timeline, and where Arshad’s experience could add value. You leave with a clear next step for assessing fit.
